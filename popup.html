Version 2.8.12. Open a Civitai gallery for automatic scrolling and collection with a live 1×–5× collection speed control and a shared 600-image queue limit. Prompt records are sent in batches of up to 60 with a 3-second pause; smaller batches are allowed when fewer are ready. Server image downloads run separately. Use the panel to stop or resume this tab, and Browsing & activity for live queue updates.
